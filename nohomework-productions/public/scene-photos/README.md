# Scene Photos Directory

This directory contains scene photos for films that can be used in film cards.

## Structure:
- `/scene-photos/donor/` - Scene photos for Donor film
- `/scene-photos/collect-call/` - Scene photos for Collect Call film
- `/scene-photos/after/` - Scene photos for After film
- `/scene-photos/christmas-movie/` - Scene photos for Christmas Movie film

## Usage:
Place scene photos in the respective film directories. The film cards will use these photos instead of poster images.

## Naming Convention:
- Use descriptive names like `scene1.jpg`, `scene2.jpg`, etc.
- Recommended formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp
- Recommended size: 400x300px or similar 4:3 aspect ratio
